# Vowel harmony and vowel length — Day 2 · Kappale 1

## Vowel harmony

Finnish vowels come in three groups:

| Back | Front | Neutral |
|---|---|---|
| a, o, u | ä, ö, y | e, i |

A word uses **either** back **or** front vowels, never both (except in compound words).
Endings change to match:

| Word has | Ending uses | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a, o, u | a | talo → talo**ssa**, Suomi → Suome**ssa** |
| ä, ö, y | ä | kylä → kylä**ssä**, työ → työ**ssä** |
| only e, i | ä | tie → tie**llä**, kieli → kiele**ssä** |

In compound words the **last part** decides: *jäätelö + kioski* → *jäätelökioski**lla***.

Same rule for **-ko/-kö**: *Puhut**ko**?* but *Syöt**kö**?*

## Short and long vowels

Length changes the meaning. Always write the double letter.

| Short | Long |
|---|---|
| tuli (fire) | tuuli (wind) |
| tili (account) | tiili (brick) |
| sika (pig) | siika (whitefish) |
| muta (mud) | muuta (other) |

Long consonants matter too: *kuka* (who) vs *kukka* (flower), *tapaan* vs *tapan*.

## Alphabet tip

Spell your name the Finnish way: A [aa], E [ee], I [ii], J [jii], K [koo], R [är], Y [yy].
