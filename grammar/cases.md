# Finnish cases (sijamuodot)

Finnish uses endings instead of prepositions. "In the house" is one word: *talossa*.
Learn them in groups, not all at once.

Example word: **talo** (house)

## Grammatical cases

| Case | Ending | talo | Meaning / use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | – | talo | basic form, subject |
| Genitive | -n | talon | of the house, owner: *talon ovi* (the house's door) |
| Partitive | -a/-ä, -ta/-tä | taloa | part of something, "some", after numbers, negatives |

## Inside cases — "in, out of, into"

| Case | Ending | talo | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inessive | -ssa/-ssä | talossa | in the house |
| Elative | -sta/-stä | talosta | out of / from the house |
| Illative | -Vn, -hVn, -seen | taloon | into the house |

## Outside cases — "on, off, onto"

| Case | Ending | talo | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adessive | -lla/-llä | talolla | at / on the house; also "having": *minulla on* (I have) |
| Ablative | -lta/-ltä | talolta | from (the surface of) the house |
| Allative | -lle | talolle | to / onto the house |

## Other common cases

| Case | Ending | talo | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essive | -na/-nä | talona | as a house (state, role) |
| Translative | -ksi | taloksi | becoming a house (change) |

## Partitive — when to use it (the hard one)

- **Uncountable things:** *Juon kahvia.* (I drink coffee.)
- **After numbers (not 1):** *kaksi kissaa* (two cats)
- **In negative sentences:** *Minulla ei ole autoa.* (I don't have a car.)
- **Unfinished actions:** *Luen kirjaa.* (I'm reading a book, not finished.)
- **Some verbs always:** *rakastaa* (love), *auttaa* (help), *odottaa* (wait)

## Vowel harmony

Endings follow the word's vowels:
- back vowels **a, o, u** → endings with **a**: *talossa*
- front vowels **ä, ö, y** → endings with **ä**: *kylässä* (in the village)
- **e, i** are neutral → they don't decide. If the word also has a/o/u, use **a**: *kirkossa* (in the church). If it has only e/i, use **ä**: *veneessä* (in the boat), *kivessä* (in the stone)
