# Personal pronouns and olla (to be) — Day 1 · Kappale 1

| Pronoun | olla | Spoken Finnish |
|---|---|---|
| minä (I) | olen | mä oon |
| sinä (you) | olet | sä oot |
| hän (he/she) | on | se on |
| me (we) | olemme | me ollaan |
| te (you pl./formal) | olette | te ootte |
| he (they) | ovat | ne on |

- **hän** is both *he* and *she*.
- **te** is plural *you*, and also the polite form for one person.
- In speech, **se** (it) is used for people and **ne** for they. Write *hän / he*, but expect to hear *se / ne*.
- The pronoun can be dropped, because the verb ending already shows the person:
  *Olen opiskelija.* = *Minä olen opiskelija.*

## Questions

Add **-ko/-kö** to the verb: *Oletko sinä opiskelija?* — *Olen.* / *En ole.*

Finnish answers yes/no by repeating the verb: *Oletko kotona? — Olen.*
