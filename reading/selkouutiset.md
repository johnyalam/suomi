# Yle Selkouutiset — reading log

News in simple Finnish: https://yle.fi/selkouutiset
Read one article, listen to the audio, and write down the words you didn't know.
Move the useful ones into a `vocab/` file afterwards so the quiz picks them up.

## How to log an article

```
## DD.MM.YYYY — article title
Link:
Summary (1–2 sentences in Finnish):

| Word | Meaning | In the article |
|---|---|---|
```
